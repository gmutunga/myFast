## 🎯 Design Philosophy

### What Makes This "Easy Fast Style"

1. **Minimalist**
   - One screen, one timer
   - Nothing distracts from tracking
   - Clean, simple interface
   - No clutter or decorations

2. **Focused**
   - Circular progress is the star
   - Large time display draws attention
   - Everything else is secondary
   - User knows exactly what to look at

3. **Professional**
   - Clean, modern typography
   - No emojis or playful decorations
   - Trustworthy appearance
   - Suitable for all ages/audiences

4. **Subtle**
   - Minimal animations (gentle pulsing)
   - Simple color scheme
   - No flashy effects
   - Calm, meditative feel

5. **Clear**
   - Large, readable numbers
   - Simple labels
   - Easy to understand at a glance
   - No confusion or complexity
